## About Me

I am a Mechatronics Engineer with an MSc in Biomedical Engineering working at the intersection of **physical systems, control, software and applied research**.

My work combines mathematical modeling, simulation, embedded implementation and software development. I am particularly interested in engineering systems where software interacts directly with physical devices, sensors, actuators, communication infrastructure or biomedical processes.

My public repositories include projects related to:

- control systems and dynamic-system analysis
- biomedical pressure regulation
- Home Assistant and home automation
- Android and Android Auto
- RTSP video infrastructure
- embedded and IoT systems
- engineering simulation and visualization
- robotics and autonomous platforms
- scientific and educational software
