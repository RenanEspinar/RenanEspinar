## About Me

I am a Mechatronics Engineer and Biomedical Engineering MSc focused on control systems, robotics, biomedical devices, embedded systems, scientific computing, prototyping and engineering education.

My work connects mathematical modeling, simulation and real-world implementation: from dynamic systems and control theory to drone configuration, biomedical pressure regulation, MATLAB educational tools, 3D printing workflows and university-level technical documentation.
